How This Works

Under the STOCK Act (2012), members of Congress and their spouses must publicly disclose stock trades within 45 days of the transaction. These disclosures are filed with the House Clerk and are publicly searchable.

This tracker compiles publicly known trades attributed to Paul Pelosi (Nancy Pelosi's husband), which are disclosed on her congressional filings. The data is sourced from official House disclosure records and widely-reported financial news.

Official disclosure search: disclosures.house.gov → Financial Disclosures → Search by Member
